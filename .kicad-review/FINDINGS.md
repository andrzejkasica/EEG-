## F-001 [HIGH] USB isolation — U9 ADuM3160 VDD1 (and PDEN/SPU) is tied to 5 V VBUS instead of the internal 3.3 V regulator output
- Source: fresh manual review (netlist and ADuM3160 datasheet Rev. D). No analyzer raised it.
- Location: usb.kicad_sch, U9 at (220.98, 58.42). Pins 1 VBUS1, 3 VDD1, 4 PDEN and 5 SPU are all on net `+5V_PRI`.
- Evidence:
  - The netlist puts U9.1 VBUS1, U9.3 VDD1, U9.4 PDEN and U9.5 SPU on `+5V_PRI`.
  - Datasheet Table 9, pin 3: "When the isolator is powered by the USB bus voltage (4.5 V to 5.5 V), the VDD1 pin should be used for a bypass capacitor to GND1. Signal lines that may require pull-up, such as PDEN and SPU, should be tied to this pin."
  - The specifications table assumes 3.1 V ≤ VDD1 ≤ 3.6 V, and says: "All logic level signals are 3.3 V and should be referenced to the local VDDx pin."
  - Forcing 5 V onto VDD1 back-drives the internal LDO output and puts side-1 operation out of spec, including the UD± drive levels (VOH max 3.6 V).
  - The absolute maximum is 6.5 V, so the part is not destroyed instantly. It is still out of spec, and enumeration and reliability are at risk.
  - The project memory note ("PDEN+SPU→VDD1 side (`+5V_PRI`)") and the STATE rationale for VM-001 ("VDD1 = 5 V side") share the same misreading. VM-001 is still a false positive.
- Fixability: mcp-approval
- Suggested fix:
  1. Disconnect U9.3 VDD1 from `+5V_PRI` and give it a new net, e.g. `VDD1_ISO1`.
  2. Add a 100 nF cap (optionally 1 µF in parallel) from that net to `GND_PRI`.
  3. Move U9.4 PDEN and U9.5 SPU onto the same new net.
  4. Keep U9.1 VBUS1 on `+5V_PRI`.

  Side 2 is fine as is: VBUS2 and VDD2 are both on +3V3, which is the datasheet's external-3.3 V option. Afterwards, the user should correct the memory note `project_power_design.md` (not a Fixer task).
- Risk if changed: low. It is a local re-wire on usb.kicad_sch; VDD1 has no other loads. SPU still matches SPD (both high means full speed).

## F-002 [HIGH] Power — TMU3-0511 is unregulated; the +5V rail can exceed the 5.5 V input limit of U2, U4 and U5
- Source: fresh manual review (TRACO TMU 3 datasheet; TPS732, TLV757P and TPS6040x input ratings).
- Location: power.kicad_sch, U7 at (48.26, 170.18). Net `+5V` feeds U5 IN/EN directly, and U2 IN and U4 IN/EN through L1.
- Evidence:
  - The TMU 3 datasheet says "Unregulated outputs". Voltage set accuracy is ±3.5 % at 60 % load. Input regulation is 1.5 % per 1 % ΔVin. Load regulation and minimum load are only given in an application note.
  - Worst case at 60 % load with VBUS = 5.25 V: 5 × 1.075 × 1.035 ≈ 5.56 V. This board draws well under 60 % of 600 mA, and at light load the output rises further.
  - TPS732 recommended VIN max is 5.5 V (SBVS037S §5.3; absolute maximum 6 V). TLV757P recommended VIN max is 5.5 V (§5.3). The TPS6040x input range is also 5.5 V max (not re-downloaded in this pass).
- Fixability: manual
- Suggested fix: measure or obtain the TMU3 light-load curve (tracopower.com/tmu3-cc), then choose one of:
  - (a) a regulated isolated converter, e.g. TRACO TMR/TEC or Murata NXE/MEE3 regulated parts;
  - (b) a low-dropout post-regulator to about 4.8–5.0 V ahead of the LDO chain (check input headroom);
  - (c) a defined preload plus a clamp, sized from the app-note curve.

  Record the decision in `project_power_design.md`.
- Risk if changed: medium. It changes the power tree and part selection, and may change the footprint and isolation rating. Re-verify against datasheets.

## F-003 [HIGH] Power — R3–R6 have value "?" and sit on the NR pins of the fixed-output LDOs U4 and U6
- Source: fresh manual review (netlist; TPS732 SBVS037S Table 4-1; TPS723 SLVS346E Table 4-1). The analyzer only flagged them indirectly (BOM value "?").
- Location: power.kicad_sch.
  - R3 (217.17, 115.57): U4 OUT → NR. R4 (217.17, 127): NR → GND. U4 is at (201.93, 113.03).
  - R5 (76.2, 116.84): U6 OUT → NR. R6 (76.2, 128.27): NR → GND. U6 is at (60.96, 115.57).
- Evidence:
  - Netlist: `Net-(U4-NR)` = R3.2, R4.1, C24, U4.4. `Net-(U6-NR)` = R5.2, R6.1, C25, U6.4.
  - TPS73225 and TPS72325 are fixed 2.5 V and −2.5 V parts. On these, pin 4 is NR: "Fixed voltage versions only—connecting an external capacitor to this pin bypasses noise generated by the internal band gap". A feedback divider only applies to the adjustable TPS73201/TPS72301.
  - A resistor on NR loads the band gap and shifts VOUT. The ADS1299 AVDD−AVSS limits are 5.5 V absolute maximum (§7.1) and 4.75–5.25 V recommended.
  - All four resistors have value "?" and no footprint. Plan Task 2 Step 4 lists "decide R3-R6".
- Fixability: manual
- Suggested fix: keep the fixed parts and delete R3–R6, leaving only the NR cap on each NR pin (see F-011). Alternatively, mark them DNP with the note "do not fit — fixed-output LDO". Only switch to the adjustable TPS73201/TPS72301 if you also calculate the divider (VFB = 1.204 V for TPS732).
- Risk if changed: low if deleted. The netlist only loses the resistor nodes.

## F-007 [MEDIUM] USB / EMC — VBUS bulk capacitance at J1 is only 100 nF
- Source: kicad-happy UC-004 plus fresh review.
- Location: usb.kicad_sch, C30 (86.36, 87.63) on `+5V_PRI`.
- Evidence:
  - The total cap on `+5V_PRI` is 0.1 µF (netlist: C30 only). USB 2.0 §7.2.4.1 expects at least 1 µF (up to 10 µF) of VBUS bypass on a device.
  - U7 is a push-pull converter with 100 mVp-p ripple. The TMU3's conducted emissions meet "EN 55032 class A (with external filter)" only.
- Fixability: mcp-approval
- Suggested fix: add a 4.7 µF X5R/X7R 0805 on `+5V_PRI`/`GND_PRI` at the U7 input. Keep the total VBUS cap ≤10 µF to respect the USB inrush limit. Optionally add the TRACO-recommended input LC filter for EMC.
- Risk if changed: low (watch the ≤10 µF inrush limit).

## F-011 [LOW] Power — NR caps C24 (1 µF) and C25 (100 nF) differ from the 10 nF datasheet value
- Source: fresh manual review (TPS732 SBVS037S, characterised with CNR = 0.01 µF; TPS723 SLVS346E §5.3, CNR nominal 0.01 µF).
- Location: power.kicad_sch, C24 (224.79, 127) on U4 NR and C25 (83.82, 128.27) on U6 NR.
- Evidence:
  - C24 = 1u is 100× the TPS732 test condition; the datasheet quotes noise with up to 0.1 µF. C25 = 100n is 10× the TPS723 nominal.
  - A larger CNR lengthens start-up, so AVDD ramps much more slowly than AVSS. This matters for the ADS1299 §11.1 sequencing check (Task 2 Step 5).
  - Components.txt gives no NR cap values.
- Fixability: manual
- Suggested fix: confirm against TI EVM SLAU443B Fig. 60 / Table 10. Otherwise set both to 10 nF C0G/X7R, or both to 100 nF for lower noise with symmetric ramps.
- Risk if changed: low (noise and start-up time only).
