## Watch out for

- **VCAP and VREF caps return to `-2V5`, not `GND`.** This is the easiest mistake on this sheet. AVSS is -2.5V in this design.
- Use `GND` everywhere on this sheet, not `GNDA`. They are merged and the netlist names the net `GND`.
- Net names must match the power sheet exactly: `+2V5`, `-2V5`, `+3V3`, `GND`. Copy a power symbol from `power.kicad_sch` if the library name differs.
- U3 stacks its repeated supply pins in the symbol: AVDD (19, 21, 22, 56, 59), AVSS (20, 23, 32, 57, 58), DVDD (48, 50), DGND (33, 49, 51). One wire per stacked group is enough.
